## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science professional from Karachi, Pakistan**. I work as a **Junior Lecturer at DHA Suffa University** (since September 2024), where I teach and mentor students and help develop lab manuals and course material. I hold a **BS in Computer Science (2020 – 2024)** and I'm currently pursuing an **MS in Computer Science**.

My main focus is **cybersecurity**. I work across both **Red Team** and **Blue Team** disciplines: penetration testing methodology, network and log analysis, incident response, and **Python security automation**. Alongside security, I build **AI-powered web applications** and enjoy **3D modeling and game development** in my free time.
